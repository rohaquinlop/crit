## Design workflow (crit)

- This project's design rules live in `design-specs/rules/`. Read them before any design task and follow them.
- Work in progress lives in `design-specs/briefs/`, finished work in `design-specs/archive/`, saved research in `design-specs/notes/`.
- Workflow: `/crit:run <request or brief name>` takes a request to work that is ready for your judgment. `/crit:wrap-up` adds what you learned to the rules. Run `/crit:setup` to change the rules.
